Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Holi·days ("we," "the site") handles information when you use this holiday countdown timer.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The countdown runs entirely in your browser using your device's local clock. We do not ask for your name, location, or any personal identifiers, and no data about which holiday you view is sent to a server.

2. Local Storage

Your light/festive theme choice may be remembered locally in your browser so it persists between visits. This value stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

3. Cookies and Advertising

This site may display advertisements served by third-party ad networks, including Google AdSense. These networks may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising via Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also review the Digital Advertising Alliance's opt-out tool at optout.aboutads.info.

4. Analytics

We may use aggregate, privacy-respecting analytics to understand overall traffic and which holidays are most popular. This data is not used to identify individual visitors.

5. Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this policy periodically. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed via the contact details on our About page.
